# Evergrow changelog

Player-facing updates, newest first. Older development recaps are reconstructed from project history, rather than individual publication dates.

## 2026-09-07 — Build your own playstyle

### New

- **Three specializations for every skill.** Discover 60 variants across melee, bows, magic and ultimates. Each skill now has three short branches with two improvements leading to a specialization. Unlock several and switch between them freely.
- **More exciting equipment.** Find mana on kill, larger area attacks, stronger dual potions, piercing projectiles, armor after blocking, and bonuses for alternating melee hits and spells.
- **Equipment that improves your skills.** Named skill bonuses can roll from +1 to +5, with higher bonuses much rarer and weapon rolls favoring matching skills.
- **Elemental hybrid builds.** Enchanted melee weapons can burn, chill or interrupt enemies. Equip a sword and wand together to alternate sword swings and magic bolts.
- **Character customization.** Change your character's appearance, armor colors and helmet visibility, with your choices saved to that character.
- **Breakable containers.** Smash crates and barrels into flying debris, sometimes uncovering gold. Elemental attacks leave their own destruction effects.
- **What's new.** Read this update and earlier development recaps directly from the main menu.

### Tweaks

- Equipment affixes have stronger identities: boots specialize in movement speed, gloves in attack speed, while amulets offer a broad range of possibilities.
- Meteor visibly falls from the sky, explodes and leaves burning ground. New specializations can extend the fire or concentrate the impact. Attack warnings now build with animated light and movement.
- Wands, staves and magical off-hands glow with their element. Staff lifts and wand flicks release bolts from their tips.
- Enemies pursue and attack more aggressively. Guardian trials bring their defenders closer to the event, and some humanoid enemies now call out during battle.
- Skill tooltips clearly name the affected skill and separate effects, costs and unlock requirements. Item and map tooltips share softer glass surfaces.
- Ground loot uses smaller labels. Hover a drop while the area is safe to inspect it; inventory tooltips compare the equipment it would replace.
- The pause menu now groups your run information with options, saving and return-to-menu actions. Maximum camera zoom-out is slightly tighter.

### Fixes

- Sword-and-wand basics alternate one action at a time instead of firing both weapons together.
- Chain Lightning no longer chooses enemies outside the visible play area.
- Enemy nameplates show active Burn, Chill and Stagger effects with remaining duration.
- Removed the extra box around item-comparison tooltips and the oversized handedness labels on item cells.
- Improved character-editor navigation with touch and controllers.

> Save notice: Characters invested in the old specialization paths may need a fresh character after this tree redesign. Their original save files are preserved.

## 2026-09-06 — A world worth exploring

> Development recap.

### New

- **Procedural crypt expeditions.** Find dungeon entrances in the world, explore connected rooms and corridors, face a powerful boss and claim its treasure.
- **Wilderness activities.** Discover guardian trials, watchtowers, graveyards, standing stones and caravan encounters, with rewards and persistent progress.
- **Goblin warbands.** Fight packs of 10–15 goblins led by tougher chiefs. Their orders and morale change the battle.
- **Journeys.** A quiet log beneath the minimap suggests nearby activities and level-appropriate destinations. Complete objectives naturally for XP, or track one on the map.
- **Skill ranks and ultimates.** Invest additional points into skills, choose a lower casting rank when mana is tight, and work toward Cataclysm, Tempest or Absolute Zero.
- **Cloud characters on the website.** Sign in with ChatGPT to continue across browsers, switch between cloud and local slots, or download and import character files.
- **Touch, controllers and handhelds.** Play with analog movement and aiming. The Android build runs offline, with a companion map and inventory on the AYN Thor's lower display.

### Tweaks

- Named danger districts, winding roads and remote wilderness give exploration a clearer sense of progression. Earlier areas keep their lower levels.
- Enemies attack independently rather than taking shared turns. Larger packs and a slower XP curve after level 4 make progression more demanding.
- Dungeons are darker, with wall torches, magical lights and glowing spell effects cutting through the shadows.
- Water, terrain, forests and character movement received visual and performance improvements. Large maps reveal detail more smoothly.
- Staff, sword and bow starting attacks were slowed to leave more room for attack- and cast-speed investment. Staff and wand basics cost a little mana.
- Controller and touch aiming provide directional assistance. The Thor companion keeps play running while you inspect items; Android rendering is limited to 60 FPS.

### Fixes

- Clearer numerals throughout the HUD, equipment details and progression screens.
- Controller A continues the selected character; handheld navigation and skill assignment are more consistent.
- The upper minimap, quest log and portal return when the Thor companion is unavailable or covered.
- Removed the pale focus overlay on Android and corrected opaque tooltip backgrounds on older WebViews.
- Improved save reliability and reduced repeated cloud uploads during play.

## 2026-09-05 — The first adventure

> Development recap.

### New

- **A living world.** Explore seven blended biomes with towns, roads, camps, roaming enemies, weathered buildings, wildlife and moving foliage.
- **Characters and equipment.** Eight character slots, a 64-item inventory, drag-and-drop equipment, attributes, procedural item names and multiple equipment rarities.
- **The skill atlas.** Thousands of connected nodes across Might, Cunning and Arcana, with weapon skills and paths for hybrid builds.
- **Town services.** Buy and sell gear at the blacksmith, gamble on jewelry, enhance equipment up to +10, reroll affixes and upgrade favorite items at the enchanter.
- **Town portals.** Channel a return to town, then take your portal back to the adventure.
- **Gold and progression.** Pick up coin piles, earn XP, gain one skill point and five attribute points per level, and see loot and discoveries in a compact notification feed.

### Tweaks

- The Astral HUD brings life and mana orbs, an XP bar, five assignable skill slots, and separate potion and dodge controls.
- A dual potion restores both life and mana. Lower starting mana regeneration makes equipment and skill choices more meaningful.
- Smooth camera zoom, wider minimap discovery, readable enemy ranks and more forgiving ranged aiming.
- Common equipment drops more frequently than rare gear early on. Item level follows the enemies and areas you fight in.
- Double-clicking a node can purchase its affordable connecting path. Hovering previews bonuses immediately.

### Fixes

- Fixed movement continuing after releasing a key during shortcuts or focus changes.
- Camp hounds no longer rapidly spin while patrolling.
- Improved weapon grips and arm alignment, inventory spacing, rarity readability and compact window headers.
- Roaming enemies appear outside the camera view instead of materializing in front of the player.
