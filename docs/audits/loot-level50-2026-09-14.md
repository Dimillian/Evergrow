# Level-50 loot audit — September 14, 2026

Source rules: `e799bfa`. Generated with `game/scripts/loot-audit.ts`. No balance changes or character saves.

## Result

Excellent drops exist, especially defensively coherent body armor and attack-speed gloves. The weapon chase is more material-driven: the top physical two-hander still wastes two affix slots on caster stats. Four Greater Affixes are extraordinarily rare, and are not required for an excellent item.

## Method

- Live reward functions; independent deterministic seeds; no rarity overrides, magic find, enhancement or rerolls.
- Equal ranks, archetypes and biomes are stratified coverage, not a player farming forecast.
- Monster level 50 yields item levels 50/51/52; rifts and Unique items are level 50.
- DPS is expected stationary basic-attack throughput including crit, without target mitigation, skill-specific effects, mana downtime or unique powers.
- 245 attribute points: 147 Strength/Intelligence/Dexterity, 98 Vitality; no tree, auras, charms or other gear. Nonweapon comparisons use one fixed level-50 common weapon.
- Armor ranks by physical EHP against level-50 hits; shield score includes expected block. This is not elemental survival.
- Sustain robe score is mana regenerated per second / mana cost multiplier. It does not price max-mana, life or damage.
- Boss archetypes are excluded from the monster sample. Unique items are counted separately and excluded from scalar best-roll rankings: their skill-changing powers are not represented by basic DPS.
- 50,000 independent kills per rank and 20,000 independent unkeyed rift completions; 265,062 equipment/charm drops plus 20,000 keys excluded from gear counts. All drops passed the runtime item validator.
- Material, base weapon family and affix quality all contribute to the ranked results. Best-in-sample is not a mathematical best-in-slot claim. Max-roll columns keep the exact same affixes, rarity, base and level; only saved roll quantiles are set to their maximum.
- The defensive metric excludes elemental damage, dodge, movement, active skills and regeneration. The DPS metric is not a full build or an encounter simulation. Compare within a category, never across melee/bow/caster fixtures.

Do not turn the combined totals into a player drop rate: the source mix is deliberately artificial. Unkeyed rifts use eight gear rolls at roughly 5% Legendary each; elite gear rolls use 0.5%. That is about 33.7% chance of at least one Legendary per unkeyed rift versus 0.62% per ordinary elite kill. Rift rewards are the primary intentional high-rarity source in this comparison, not a reason by itself to increase rarity.

## The four-Greater-Affix drop

**Graveglass Requiem — Linen Shroud**, Legendary, level 50, seed `964196502` (unkeyedRift).

- ✦ intelligence: +12
- ✦ lifeRegen: +8
- ✦ manaRegen: +12
- ✦ maxHp: +190

Only **1 ordinary equipment item with four Greater Affixes** appeared among **8,439 Legendaries** (and 265,062 total equipment/charm drops). This particular result is not a stable estimated probability: only one observation. Four independent continuous rolls above the 90th percentile would be 1 in 10,000 before asking for a specific base, affix combination or rarity; discrete ranks/pierce cannot be Greater Affixes.

## Audit findings and proposed priorities

1. **Reduce unproductive melee combinations first.** 3,615 of 5,707 unenchanted Epic/Legendary melee weapons (63.3%) rolled Intelligence and/or spell damage. These do not increase their physical weapon damage; Intelligence can still supply mana. Keep deliberate hybrids possible, especially on elemental weapons, rather than blanket-removing spell bonuses.
2. **Rebalance the importance of weapon rolls relative to material.** The winning Crystal Greataxe produces 2,597.92 expected basic DPS in this fixture, versus 2,226.64 with the same base/rarity and no affixes: only +16.7%. Half its affixes are caster stats. Its Crystal material scales base damage by 1.55 versus Iron. The winning gloves improve their fixture by +63.3%. Preserve rare-material excitement, but consider moving some offensive budget toward weapon-specific useful rolls instead of another global damage buff.
3. **Keep the armor chase.** The winning Iron Cuirass has +336 life, +29 Vitality, +253 armor and +9 life/sec. Life plus Vitality adds 510 maximum life. Its static physical EHP is +117.4% over the identical affixless base and +31.1% over the sampled median Legendary metal chest. This is an excellent four-affix combination without requiring the rarest material.
4. **Decide whether robes are meant to be a damage chase or a resource chase.** The best offensive robe adds about 13.5% fixture DPS; the sustain robe supplies +8 mana/5 sec and 13% reduced costs. Both matter, but offer different payoffs. Current robes can roll spell damage, Intelligence, mana, regeneration, cost reduction, life and armor; chest pieces cannot roll skill ranks. Add skill-specific robe identity only if desired rather than raising every robe stat.
5. **Treat coherent combinations plus one or two Greater Affixes as excellent loot.** Epic equipment has three random affixes; Legendary has four. A five-stat perfect Legendary cannot exist with the current rules. Greater Affixes mark top-decile roll quality, not build suitability or a separate power multiplier. Rounding and caps can also make a non-Greater value equal to the maximum printed value. The best weapon examples do not need Greater Affixes to win.
6. **Keep caster, bow and hybrid options.** A Crystal Star Wand reached +46% spell damage, +9 Intelligence, +6 mana/5 sec and +3% crit. A Crystal Longbow combined attack damage, Strength, Dexterity and crit. The coherent hybrid Crystal Grave Maul (seed 1652872447) rolled +38% attack damage, +12 Strength, +40 local lightning and +32% spell damage; the spell bonus really does scale its added lightning. It scored 2,544.96 expected basic DPS, close to the winning greataxe, and demonstrates an intentional hybrid can work.
7. **No immediate blanket rarity increase.** The reward sample already includes 8,439 Legendaries and 8,371 Uniques. Source-normalized acquisition, good-affix coherence and base/affix budget balance should be tuned separately. Uniques are fixed designs, not random perfect-roll items; evaluating their full strengths requires their specific skills and encounter behavior.

8. **The displayed gear power cannot identify great rolls.** `deriveItem` computes power from level, rarity, material scale and affix count, without using affix identities or roll values. Two otherwise identical pieces with minimum and maximum rolls receive the same power score. Build-aware comparison should remain authoritative; revise how the score is presented or calculated before treating it as a quality guide.
