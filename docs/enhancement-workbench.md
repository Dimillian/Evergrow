# Enhancement workbench · local 2026-09-22

The workbench groups the selected item, exact stat gains, gold receipt and paid action in the left pane. The right pane shares the inventory page’s head slot and two equipment rails, with the shared character portrait between them. Inventory / Charms tabs below it filter the existing spatial pack while preserving item positions and per-category overflow. Two-handed gear reserves the off-hand slot. The Enhance button names its destination rank; the adjacent receipt shows the full quoted price and remaining balance, or the exact shortage. There is no second payment action in the far-right window footer. Clicking the displayed workbench item clears its selection and quote, leaving the gear in its original equipped or inventory slot. Hover still inspects it; selection is held during charging/saving, with Cancel available during the charge.

The item rests inside a bounded procedural steel-and-brass engraving, sharing the window's palette. Its complete diamond, corner strokes and seals stay inside the SVG viewBox; the former oversized rotated halo and its clipped edge are removed. Current and Next captions keep the main comparison fixed on the owned rank and next useful upgrade. The current rank is the larger readout. A quiet +0 through +10 progress rail marks completed, current and next ranks; it is not a target selector. Hover, keyboard focus or touch opens the shared retained tooltip. Each tooltip lists only that rank’s incremental stat gains against the preceding rank (for example, Armor +1 or Life +2), never cumulative gains against the currently owned item. +0 is the base rank; rounded-away steps show no gain and retain the free-skip rule. Arrow keys, Home and End move tooltip focus without changing the main comparison, quote or action. Escape dismisses the tooltip first. The empty workbench omits meaningless progress marks and price placeholders. A coin emblem, restrained engraved button ends and a square jade check complete the shared material treatment. Charge and impact effects stay within the item stage, including narrow layouts.

## Research and design choices

- **Black Desert:** its [official enhancement guide](https://blackdesert.pearlabyss.com/Console/en-US/Game/Wiki?_masterWikiNo=314) separates materials, success probability, selected equipment and failure consequences. Its [October 2025 UI redesign](https://blackdesert.pearlabyss.com/TR/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=18288) makes gear selection immediate, groups enhancement resources and reduces repeated confirmations. Our interpretation: keep the transaction's item, price and certainty adjacent, with one prominent action.
- **Lost Ark:** its [March 2024 honing improvements](https://www.playlostark.com/en-us/game/releases/break-through-to-thaemine) allow the honing animation to end on a click and reuse support materials for the next attempt. [Guardians' Rage](https://www.playlostark.com/en-gb/game/releases/guardians-rage) further shortens progression-result animations and adds bulk options. Our interpretation: make the first reveal satisfying while keeping repeat upgrades quick and skippable.

These references inform interaction and pacing, not copied artwork or balance. Evergrow's existing square metalwork, typography and shared procedural item art remain the visual foundation.

Every merchant tab’s equipment section includes the inventory’s shared animated character portrait between the slot rails, with the head slot above. It reflects current appearance, equipped items and earned weapon glow. Its reserved height keeps the bag toolbar aligned across merchant tabs. Rendering stops when scrolled out of view, hidden or closed; reduced motion freezes the pose.

## Rhythm and outcome

1. **Read:** current → next rank, changed bonuses, upgrade cost and remaining balance. Rounded-away ranks still skip at no extra charge. Instructional filler and redundant headings are removed.
2. **Charge:** a 1.6-second gold bar, eighteen bounded gold/jade embers drifting along irregular paths with staggered starts and gathering around the item, growing glow and a short heating sound. Cancel or closing the window discards the pending attempt without submitting a purchase. Other item/tab actions are held during this operation.
3. **Commit:** the Web Animation’s finished promise releases the existing durable trade only at the final charge tick. The bar holds just below completion while persistence settles. A transaction already submitted for persistence cannot be cancelled.
4. **Impact:** only after commitment, the bar completes, the item catches a brief gold light, twelve bounded sparks and two expanding diamond outlines disperse, and a metallic low impact resolves into a short chord on completion.
5. **Receipt:** the completed rank and actual gained stats stay visible, with the next upgrade ready. Reaching +10 shows “Fully enhanced.” No extra acknowledgement click or duplicate notification is required.

“Skip animation” persists on this device under `evergrow-enhancement-skip-v1`, outside character saves. Enabling it during a charge finishes that charge immediately; persistence still precedes the reveal. Reduced motion uses the instant presentation and static receipt. Unavailable preference storage keeps the setting for the session and says so. The normal master, mute, foreground and SFX settings govern sounds.

All blacksmith enhancements through **+10 are guaranteed**. Rounded-away ranks skip for free, charging one fee from the current rank. The original price curve is restored: `ceil(3 × level budget × rarity × material service multiplier × 1.65^currentRank)`. A level-10 Rare iron item with no free skips costs 77,504 gold for +9 → +10 and 195,428 gold total from +0 → +10. Rarity/level increases and affix rerolls retain their existing rules.

The commerce planner validates the quote and stages the upgrade and gold debit atomically. No enhancement roll, failure penalty or chance metadata remains. Only a successful save publishes the upgrade; transaction/save rejection leaves gear and gold unchanged. No save fields or migrations are needed.

## Ownership and verification

`EnhancementSequence` gates the existing trade behind a cancellable visual charge. It never changes gold, item recipes, RNG or saves itself. Duplicate submissions are held. Cancellation and disposal before the final tick prevent the transaction entirely; after that boundary, save rejection remains an ordinary commerce error. Session identity prevents an old completion from appearing in a reopened panel. The runtime uses the bar’s actual Web Animation completion; headless tests inject a deterministic final-tick scheduler.

The existing Equipment → Enhancement workbench review opens `/services.html?operation=enhance&study`. Replay resets disposable gear and triggers the actual panel action. Pause/resume, ½×/¼× playback and a charge scrubber control the real Web Animations and CSS effects; resuming from the end crosses the normal final-tick commitment boundary. Longsword, shield, armor and charm fixtures support +0–10, ready, no selection, insufficient gold, fully enhanced and simulated save-error states. Optional sound and configuration are URL-addressable. Hidden pages pause motion, reduced motion follows the OS, and the Skip animation setting stays in memory. The tool never accesses playable character saves. Gameplay/economy acceptance remains with the player.

Headless coverage checks no commitment before the final tick, cancellation/retry, fast/slow saves, duplicate calls, skipping before persistence, rejection/throw, disposal/reopen and preference reload/storage failure. Every-rank derivation covers regular, starter and unique gear, next-effective-rank parity and non-mutation. Tooltip tests verify that each delta compares adjacent ranks, is independent of the owned rank and preserves empty steps without changing free skips. Commerce tests cover guaranteed upgrades across ranks and item kinds, useful-rank skips, restored prices, stale/forged quotes, insufficient funds, atomic saved upgrades and rollback on rejected saves.

## Earned enhancement glow

Weapon ranks +6–+10 add increasingly active warm-gold effects: +6 gets a pulsing halo and moving surface sheen; +7 adds two drifting embers; +8 adds four embers and a travelling light filament; +9 uses six embers and two interweaving filaments; +10 uses nine embers, three filaments, the strongest white-gold rim and three staggered star flares. +0–+5 retain their existing art. The glow follows the owned recipe rank and changes only after an upgrade commits. `enhancement-art.ts` owns the shared five-rank progression; icon filters have unique local resource IDs and stay within the existing art envelope.

Square icons, spatial pack art and the forge use the same SVG treatment for weapons, including Unique weapons. Main-hand and off-hand weapons carry their individual ranks through the shared world, portrait and appearance-editor rig. Ground weapon art uses the same local Canvas glow. Armor, cloaks, shields, foci, jewelry and charms receive no earned enhancement glow. Their stats and one-shot forge animations are unchanged. Existing material, tint and elemental effects remain independently visible; unequipping a weapon removes its glow.

The earned finish runs continuously while visible. SVG uses CSS animation, a silhouette-masked light sweep and fixed ember/trail elements. Canvas adds the sweep and fixed procedural spark lanes after the material, using the renderer’s existing effect clock; the effect has no particle history, Canvas blur, independent timer or world-light emitter. The five rank profiles share timing/counts across both presentations. Reduced motion freezes Canvas effects and keeps the SVG’s still halo/rim; no continuous spark motion remains. Existing material and rarity colors remain intact; no item/save fields, stats, costs or odds change. Portrait bounds reserve space for the halo. Use the enhancement study’s Rank control to compare +5 through +10; the Fully enhanced preset shows the cap immediately. Replay glow restarts the selected item’s cosmetic loop without buying an upgrade. Pause/resume and speed control both the earned finish and forge action. Infinite cosmetic loops do not keep the development toolbar’s polling frame alive. The earned motion hides during charging/saving so the one-shot forge animation stays legible. Skip animation remains the preference for the paid upgrade sequence, not a toggle for earned cosmetics.
